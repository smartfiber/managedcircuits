Renewal guide

Do not let the carrier’s deadline become your strategy.

Start early enough to understand the service, the contract, the market, and the alternatives.

Collect the operating facts

  • Current invoices, contracts, amendments, and service schedules
  • Locations, circuit IDs, bandwidth, access types, and providers
  • Renewal date, notice window, auto-renewal language, and termination rights
  • Performance history, outages, support issues, and unresolved billing items
  • Planned openings, closures, moves, growth, cloud changes, and application needs

Benchmark before negotiating

Evaluate current market pricing, carrier availability, construction exposure, replacement technology, flexibility, and resiliency—not just the incumbent’s first renewal offer.

Make the renewal a business decision

Align the next term with operational goals, growth plans, risk tolerance, and technology direction.
