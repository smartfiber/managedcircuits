Procurement checklist

Ten questions to answer before renewing a circuit

A renewal should be a business decision—not an automatic extension triggered by a carrier notice.

  1. Is the service still the right access type?

    Compare the existing circuit against current fiber, broadband, fixed-wireless, and cellular options.

  2. Has the market price changed?

    Benchmark comparable providers, speeds, terms, and installation risk.

  3. Does the location need more resilience?

    Review downtime impact, backup design, physical diversity, and failover behavior.

  4. Are you paying for unused features?

    Audit managed equipment, IP blocks, voice components, and ancillary fees.

  5. Is the term aligned with the site plan?

    Avoid contracts that outlive leases, planned relocations, or technology migrations.

  6. Are renewal and cancellation windows documented?

    Confirm notice requirements and auto-renewal language before the deadline.

  7. Is the billing accurate?

    Validate taxes, surcharges, installation credits, and the contracted monthly rate.

  8. Has service performance justified renewal?

    Review outages, repair experience, escalation effectiveness, and SLA credits.

  9. Can the provider support your broader footprint?

    Consider whether standardization creates leverage or introduces concentration risk.

  10. Who owns the renewal decision?

    Assign the business, technical, commercial, and legal approvals before urgency takes over.
